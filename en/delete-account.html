Delete your account and data

App: Waytale.

In the app

  • Delete all data. Profile → "Delete all data". This erases explored areas, paths, timeline and statistics from the phone. If you are signed in, you can also erase them from the server.
  • Delete the account. Profile → account → "Delete account". All your data on the server and the account itself are deleted at once.
  • Delete one day's path. In the Timeline, open the day and tap "Delete". The path is removed from the server and from friends you shared it with.

Without the app

Write to comeahead.dev@gmail.com from the email of your Google account. The account and all its data will be deleted.
